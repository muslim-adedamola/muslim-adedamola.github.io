I am a PhD student at the Chair of Cyber-Physical Systems at Montanuniversität Leoben, supervised by Prof. Elmar Rückert. My research lies at the intersection of robot learning, reinforcement learning, and embodied AI, with a focus on dexterous manipulation and generalist robot policies. Previously, I completed my MS in Robotics at Nazarbayev University and conducted research at the AI for Robotics Lab, University of Zagreb.
